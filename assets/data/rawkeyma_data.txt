Full Description:

Rawkemya is a professional B2B marketplace designed for the industrial chemical sector, offering direct access to premium raw materials and verified formulations. It bridges the gap between suppliers and manufacturers by providing detailed technical data, real-time price tracking, and a streamlined procurement experience.

Designed for manufacturers, chemists, and industrial professionals, Rawkemya enables users to explore a comprehensive catalog of raw materials with full technical specifications, including CAS numbers and safety-related information. The platform goes beyond sourcing by offering a specialized library of “Top Formulations” and professional recipes across multiple industries such as cosmetics, paints, and detergents.

With integrated real-time communication tools, users can connect directly with verified suppliers, request quotations, and manage procurement efficiently from a single interface. Rawkemya empowers businesses to optimize production, reduce sourcing time, and stay competitive in the industrial chemical market.

Why choose Rawkemya?

Rawkemya is more than just a sourcing platform — it is a complete industrial solution that combines procurement, technical knowledge, and supplier communication in one place. Whether you are sourcing raw materials or developing new formulations, Rawkemya provides the tools needed to enhance productivity and decision-making.
